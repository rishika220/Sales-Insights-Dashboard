# Sales-Insights-Dashboard
An interactive Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze and visualize sales performance across different business dimensions.

Description
   
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and visualize sales performance, profit, quantity, regional trends, customer segments, payment methods, and shipping modes. It helps transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

Tech Stack
   
Microsoft Power BI
Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Data Visualization
Microsoft Bing Maps

Data Source
   
Sales dataset containing order details, sales, profit, quantity, region, segment, payment mode, shipping mode, and product sub-categories.
Dataset source: Not specified (replace with the actual dataset source if available).

Features / Highlights
   
Sales KPI Cards: Total Sales (1.57M), Total Quantity (22K), Total Profit (175.26K), and Average Delivery Days.
Regional Analysis: Interactive region filters for Central, East, South, and West.
Segment Analysis: Sales breakdown by Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office.
Monthly Sales Trends: Year-wise sales comparison (2019 vs 2020).
Profit Analysis: Monthly profit trends across different years.
Shipping Mode Analysis: Sales comparison by Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day.
Payment Mode Analysis: Sales distribution across COD, Online, and Cards.
Product Category Analysis: Sales comparison across Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture.
Sub-Category Analysis: Performance of Phones, Chairs, Binders, Storage, and other products.
Interactive Visualizations: Dynamic charts, filters, and geographical sales mapping.

Business Impact & Insights

This dashboard helps businesses understand sales performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

Key Business Insights

Customer Segment:   Consumer segment contributes the highest sales (approximately 48%), followed by Corporate and Home Office.
Regional Performance: Enables comparison of sales across four regions to identify strong and weak markets.
Shipping Insights: Standard Class generates the highest sales (0.42M), indicating its major contribution to shipping-mode sales.
Payment Trends: COD contributes the largest share of sales (approximately 42.6%), followed by Online and Cards.
Product Performance: Office Supplies leads with 0.64M in sales, followed by Technology and Furniture.
Top Sub-Categories: Phones, Chairs, and Binders are among the leading sub-categories.
Sales & Profit Trends: Monthly and yearly comparisons help identify performance trends and fluctuations.

Business Impact

Supports data-driven business decisions.
Helps identify top-performing customer segments and products.
Provides better visibility into regional sales performance.
Helps businesses understand customer payment preferences.
Supports shipping and operational performance analysis.
Highlights potential opportunities for improving sales and profitability.
